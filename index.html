I build the tooling that makes hard problems legible.

Cloud support engineer at AWS, working on generative-AI services. I reproduce before I answer, and I build the diagnostic tools I wish I had.

My route here was not a straight line. I trained as an architect, spent six years as a graphic and UX designer across Seoul, Brazil and Sydney, taught myself Python to contribute to a health-tech product API, and retrained formally into cloud. The design years are not a detour I explain away — they are why I care whether an interface, a diagram or an error message actually lands.

Day to day I work deep-dive customer cases on Amazon Bedrock, Bedrock AgentCore and SageMaker for regulated enterprises. The work I am proudest of is the tooling around it.
